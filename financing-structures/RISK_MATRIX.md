# Risk allocation matrix
Design checklist, not provider terms.

| Risk | Mechanism to test | Residual risk / cost |
|---|---|---|
| Completion | Dated EPC; contingency; sponsor support; performance bond | Contractor credit; interfaces; cost premium |
| Demand/offtake | Creditworthy contract; collection controls; reserves | Volumes; tariff; enforcement |
| Sovereign/regulatory | Contract allocation; eligible political risk cover | Exclusions; claims delays; guarantor credit |
| Credit default | Priced partial guarantee; junior capital | Retained losses; correlation; claims timing |
| FX | Matched currency; indexing; priced hedge | Basis; convertibility; uncovered tail |
| Liquidity/refinance | Amortisation; reserve; matched tenor | Secondary market uncertainty |
| Technology/ESG | Independent expert; proven equipment; action plans | Remediation; reputational and tail risks |

Potential specialist map from the papers: ATIDI, AGF, GuarantCo/PIDG, MIGA, Afreximbank, AfDB, IFC, TDB and relevant ECAs. Dhamana has been added to the facility interface map after primary-source review. Confirm each mandate, transaction eligibility, coverage, premium and exclusions. These are candidates, not committed providers.
