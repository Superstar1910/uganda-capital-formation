# Decision rights
| Decision | Accountable role |
|---|---|
| Policy/fiscal appropriation | Government/MoFPED within lawful authority |
| Regulation/supervision | Relevant regulator |
| Coordination and scorecard | Proposed NCFC; no transaction approval |
| Facility budget and procurement | Independent facility/platform board |
| Project preparation | Facility operator and contracted specialists |
| Asset underwriting/valuation | Mandated licensed manager and independent specialists |
| Scheme allocation | Trustees and their mandated managers |
| Guarantee issuance | Provider’s independent approval; fiscal authority for public exposure |
| Project close | Sponsor for project; each investor for own funds |
| Public research release | Research maintainer and independent reviewer |

Separate related-party advisory and investment mandates. Declare fees/ownership; independent selection; recusal; sponsor consent for disclosure. National priorities do not direct pension allocation.
