# Hybrid infrastructure and capital recycling
The 2019 GCIFP/Ortus concept was refined under Oakstone in August 2026: prepare candidate note issuers, deploy hybrid risk capital, and pursue operating-asset refinancing through notes, bonds, listings or institutional take-out.

Retain the function; revalidate the wrapper. Prior US$1bn-equivalent size, 15–21% gross IRR and US$10–25m TA range are legacy design assumptions, not current market terms or commitments. Open-ended growth ambition does not justify liquid redemption against illiquid assets. Vehicle form, dealing windows, valuation, leverage, investor eligibility and exit must be reviewed together.

Risk equity/specialist lenders may finance development and construction. Only once assets operate and cash flows are evidenced should pension refinancing be tested. Refinancing can release development capital but does not equal new capex; report both separately.

A fund, TA facility and bond programme require separate accounts and decision rights. No guaranteed public-money multiplier is assumed. Every leverage claim needs denominator, period, eligible proceeds, disbursement status and fiscal exposure.
