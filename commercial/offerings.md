# Oakstone commercial offerings
## Capital Formation Diagnostic
Customers: public agencies, DFIs, banks, institutional investors. Scope: mandate, capital inventory, deployment constraints, institution/facility map, financing gap and prioritised roadmap. Outputs: diagnostic, evidence register, model and executive workshop. Proposed engagement 4–6 weeks, adjusted to scope. Fixed fee; data gaps and implementation excluded unless agreed.
## Project Bankability Accelerator
Customers: sponsors and project-preparation programmes. Scope: screen pipeline, confirm demand and readiness, build models, allocate risks and develop financier materials. Outputs: readiness scorecard, financing model, diligence gaps and lender pack. Milestone pricing; technical/legal work coordinated with specialists and priced separately. Financing success fees only under a separate mandate where permitted.
## Alternatives Investment Support
Customers: pension schemes, insurers and regulated fund managers. Scope: strategy, eligible pipeline, diligence framework and monitoring. Outputs: policy options, assessment memos and portfolio reporting. Retainer plus scoped assignments; investment authority and regulatory responsibilities remain expressly allocated.
## Commercial controls
No invented price benchmarks. Quote after validating procurement route, deliverables, effort, taxes, third-party costs and responsibilities. Disclose sponsorship and conflicts. Never sell favourable research rankings. No public disclosure of client pipeline without consent.
## First commercial cycle
Prepare a sample diagnostic using synthetic data; approach institutions with a concise briefing; secure a paid pilot; collect feedback; standardise recurring deliverables. Build subscriptions only after repeat demand. Fund creation requires separate anchor capital, governance and authorisation.
