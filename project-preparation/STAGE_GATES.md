# Stage-gate manual
Two lanes: operating assets for earlier demonstration; greenfield assets for longer preparation and specialist development-risk capital. Same conflict and evidence standards for public/private referrals.

| Gate | Decision evidence | Budget/risk owner | Stop test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Strategic fit | Sponsor standing; lawful revenue; sector; minimum scale | Origination unit | Ineligible or unmanaged conflict |
| 1 Preliminary viability | Demand; pre-feasibility; title path; indicative economics | Facility and sponsor | Negative economics or no risk owner |
| 2 Bankability | Feasibility; technical/legal/ESG DD; contracts; model | TA and approved development capital | Unfixable permissions or no equity |
| 3 Investment readiness | Data room; procurement; term sheets; risk quotes | Facility and sponsor agreement | Net hurdle/governance failure |
| 4 Independent approval | Manager IC and investor/trustee decisions | Each investor | Concentration or liquidity breach |
| 5 Financial close | Signed contracts; CPs; security; drawdown monitoring | Transaction parties | Material outstanding CP |

Each gate has owner, evidence index, fee cap, procurement route, independent challenge and kill memorandum. Pre-agree eligible recovery at successful close. Failure losses remain with designated development-risk capital. Report failed projects in anonymised cohorts.
