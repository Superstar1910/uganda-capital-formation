# First 100 days — retirement platform diagnostic
Recommended plan, not booked activities or an authorised mandate.

| Days | Work | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1–15 | Convenor and charter boundaries | No-direction and conflicts protocol |
| 1–30 | Independent legal/regulatory mapping | Instrument eligibility and approvals |
| 15–45 | Anonymised scheme/liability baseline | Allocation and five-year liquidity capacity |
| 15–55 | Trustees/managers/insurers interviews | Tickets; hurdles; tenor; governance |
| 20–60 | Existing-facility and pipeline scan | Referral map and 8–12 initial screens |
| 30–70 | Facility design and recovery | Charter; procurement; sponsor agreement |
| 40–75 | DFIs/guarantors/TA soundings | Non-binding fit and indicative terms |
| 60–85 | Independent pilot selection | 2–4 budgeted Gate 1 candidates |
| 75–100 | Costed operating plan | Go/no-go; owners; funding; publication |

Acceptance: lawful accountable owner; independent trustees; at least three scheme demand profiles; an asset plausibly capable of Gate 2 within 12 months; funded preparation losses; bounded fiscal liabilities. These are design tests, not achieved milestones.

Meetings proposed: URBRA; MoFPED fiscal and financial-sector teams; NSSF; occupational schemes; licensed managers including the previously contemplated Old Mutual role; CMA; UDB; UDC; BoU; insurers; preparation funders; DFIs; guarantee providers; counsel/actuary; sponsors. No emails or invitations are sent by this repository.
