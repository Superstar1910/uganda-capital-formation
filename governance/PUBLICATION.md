# Publication governance
Every observation needs a source, observation period, unit, price basis, retrieval date and verification status. Distinguish official targets, observed data, estimates, scenarios, commitments and funded disbursements. Missing data are null, not zero.

Contributor proposes changes with evidence; maintainer checks sources and calculations; independent reviewer clears externally relied-upon numerical releases. Publish change log and revision impact. Conflicted contributors disclose interests and recuse from commercial project rankings. Keep personal data and confidential mandates outside public source. Record dataset licences before redistribution.

Monthly data review and quarterly synthesis are proposed cadences, not active automations. Source failures preserve prior observations with stale flags. Do not automatically publish unreviewed AI-generated conclusions.
