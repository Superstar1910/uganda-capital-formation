# Uganda Capital Formation
An open research and implementation framework connecting Uganda’s US$500bn by 2040 ambition to productive investment. Convened by Oakstone Capital. **Edition 0.2 — project-informed programme.** Independent initiative; proposed institutional roles are not appointments, and no government mandate or financing commitment is asserted.

## Explore
Open `dist/index.html` for the scenario laboratory and `dist/programme.html` for programme architecture. No installation required. HTML is portable to a static host. Download annual calculations, assumptions and the framework from the site.

## Integrated project work
Source register: `data/provenance/project-work-register.csv`. Twenty project-folder artifacts reviewed, plus the earlier June strategy and project decisions. The programme integrates:
- ten national workstreams and seven platform concepts;
- proposed MoFPED policy host, UDB execution anchor, non-regulatory NCFC, contemplated Old Mutual role, Oakstone advisory/PMO and GCIFP product-development lineage;
- preparation, de-risking and anchor-capital engines;
- pension fiduciary/eligibility/liquidity discipline and independent managers;
- Hybrid Infrastructure Fund preparation/investment/bond-recycling architecture;
- ATMS origination, arrears, market infrastructure and mineral-linked investability;
- facility synergies, scoped 90/100-day plans and commercial economics.

## Run
`python models/scenario.py` reproduces the strategy illustration. `python models/article_scenario.py` reproduces the September article’s piecewise investment path. `python models/facility_economics.py` demonstrates recoverable-preparation cash flows. See individual model notes. Run `python -m unittest discover -s tests` for meaningful arithmetic/reconciliation checks.

## Limits and publication
Strategy illustration defaults are USD50bn/2023 → USD500bn/2040; rates are scenarios. The article’s USD62bn/2025 baseline and investment ramps are inherited research, not a refreshed actual release. Conflicting versions are preserved in `data/provenance/conflicts.csv`. No current national pipeline, legal opinion or deployable capital total is asserted. Confidential original documents and client data are excluded. Public repository: https://github.com/Superstar1910/uganda-capital-formation.
