# Comparator research agenda
Two separate questions: investment/growth outcomes and institutional mechanisms.

## Outcomes
Reproduce WDI GCF and real-growth series for Uganda, Viet Nam, Malaysia, Kenya and South Africa. Common observation windows, definitions and null handling. Historic averages in the article remain prior calculations pending refreshed verification. Investment share divided by growth is a sensitivity heuristic, not a causal coefficient.

## Mechanisms
KfW: mandate segmentation and market funding; BNDES/KDB: industrial and long-tenor finance; Singapore/Khazanah: professional state investment; BRD: targeted development banking. Pension/asset-management mechanisms in Kenya, South Africa, Nigeria, Australia and Canada need current regulator rules, audited reports and realised cases.

For each: problem, mechanism, institutional authority, funding, investor risk, actual results, failures and Ugandan transfer limits. Avoid copying foreign legal wrappers or pension allocation targets. Do not recycle prior deck balance-sheet numbers as current facts.
