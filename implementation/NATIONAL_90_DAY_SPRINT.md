# National programme 90-day design sprint
Separate from the retirement-capital 100-day diagnostic.
1. Confirm policy host and proposed UDB execution role; establish non-regulatory coordination.
2. Audit GDP/investment baseline and classify previous claims.
3. Map existing facilities, mandates, duplications and referral pathways.
4. Scope seven platforms and decide which can use existing institutions.
5. Screen sector pipelines, including mineral-linked infrastructure and exports.
6. Build national need, capital supply, pipeline and fiscal-risk ledgers.
7. Define investor demand, professional management and pilot financing routes.
8. Deliver costed programme, legal work plan, public scorecard and go/no-go memorandum.
No inference of Government approval, appointment or financing is permitted.
