# National guarantee coordination window

Status: proposed concept, not launched or funded. Source: National programme / retirement paper.

Market function: Aggregate demand for existing risk capacity.

Mechanism: Risk-specific cover; portfolio structures; fiscal ledger.

Required design tests: Provider eligibility; claims; pricing; exclusions; no blanket guarantee.

Next decision: validate existing providers, demand, legal route, cost and risk-adjusted investment case before forming a new vehicle. No partner appointment or commitment is implied.
