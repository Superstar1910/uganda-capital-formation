# Experience informs design; it does not establish a funded national platform
The project executive summary records Old Mutual alternatives advisory, Yield Uganda formation/supervision, UKNIAF/MIIA/DFC preparation and market work, Ethiopia CAFF fund/TA architecture, infrastructure lender diligence and sponsor-side energy/industrial development.

Reusable lessons:
- Fund design needs governance, manager, pipeline and liabilities together.
- Preparation is a budgeted investment function with rejection gates.
- Technical assistance and post-investment supervision must connect.
- Guarantees transfer defined risks and can reduce net yield after premiums.
- Financing committee approval or term sheet differs from financial close and drawdown.
- A proposed fund/programme size differs from capital raised or deployed.

This release uses design lessons rather than publishing confidential transaction packs or a revalidated personal track record. Named project capacities, amounts, roles and permission to disclose require source-record checks; the source records themselves remain in controlled storage.
