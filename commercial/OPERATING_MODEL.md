# Public framework and commercial operating model
This builds on the project’s existing separation of public-good market development, transaction preparation and commercial investment management.

| Function | Potential payer | Receipt | Economic test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public data/standards/trustee capability | Industry; lawful public budget; development partner | Grant or commissioned programme | Public value and budget scrutiny |
| Facility team/screening | Programme sponsors; partner grants; disclosed sponsor fees | Contract fees/grant | Lean overhead and competitive access |
| Transaction studies | Sponsor co-pay; TA; risk-bearing preparation capital | Pre-agreed close recovery | Portfolio failures, delay and recovery |
| Deal advisory | Sponsor/procuring authority | Retainer and milestones; permitted success fee | Conflicts and net customer value |
| Asset management | Consenting investors | Disclosed management/performance fees | Authorisation, net return and fiduciary oversight |

Oakstone’s three initial offers remain diagnostics, bankability and institutional alternatives support. Add programme PMO and vehicle-development mandates as execution extensions. Analytics, training and subscriptions scale only from validated recurring customer needs.

Do not assume preparation recovery covers every failed study. The prior retirement paper’s US$0.4–0.8m diagnostic, US$2–5m setup and 1–3% study reference were preliminary envelopes; no procurement-backed budget or fee benchmark is adopted. The five-year model should show grants, sponsor co-pay, burn, failure rate, recovery delays and runway. Management fees start only on an actual managed-capital mandate.
