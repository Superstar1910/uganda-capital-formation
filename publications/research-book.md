# Expanded research edition

The browser edition is maintained in `dist/research.html`. This is the canonical substantive synthesis; the earlier inspection pages are historical material.

## 1. Purpose, audience and measurable outcomes

<p>This project is a public working framework for turning Uganda’s growth ambition into productive assets, investable projects and accountable delivery. The $500bn by 2040 endpoint is an ambition used for scenario design; it is not a forecast or a financing commitment.</p><div class="table"><table><thead><tr><th>Audience</th><th>Contribution sought</th><th>First useful outcome</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Government and public agencies</td><td>Policy ownership, project data, procurement and fiscal decisions</td><td>A named sponsor and a legally authorised pilot</td></tr><tr><td>Pension trustees, insurers and asset managers</td><td>Liability constraints, underwriting criteria and independent investment decisions</td><td>A mandate-compatible investment screen</td></tr><tr><td>Sponsors and businesses</td><td>Project information, sponsor equity and execution responsibility</td><td>A project that passes a preparation gate</td></tr><tr><td>DFIs, guarantors and climate funders</td><td>Preparation support and priced risk instruments</td><td>An eligibility opinion and indicative terms</td></tr><tr><td>Researchers, citizens and practitioners</td><td>Evidence, criticism, technical work and monitoring</td><td>A traceable correction or tested method</td></tr></tbody></table></div><p>Success means independently reviewed projects reaching close, additional productive expenditure, transparent risk allocation and learning from rejected projects. Website visits and expressions of interest are engagement measures, not capital mobilised.</p>

## 2. A consistent macro framework

<p>Maintain four separate ledgers: national accounts investment; transaction financing; institutional asset stocks; and preparation expenditure. A pension asset stock cannot be added to annual investment, a guarantee cannot be counted as funded cash, and refinancing does not automatically create new capital formation.</p><p>The inherited article scenario starts at $62bn GDP and a 22.65% investment rate in 2025. Its checkpoints are $120bn/30% in 2030, $250bn/35% in 2035 and $500bn/40% in 2040. Straight-line interpolation of GDP and rates gives $1,226.976bn cumulative investment during 2026–2040 and $393.216bn above a 24% comparison rate on the same GDP path. These are reproducible historical assumptions, not refreshed actuals or evidence that investment alone causes this growth.</p><p>The separate constant-growth model starts at $50bn in 2023. Do not splice its annual values into the article model. From $62bn in 2025 to $500bn in 2040 requires approximately 14.9% annual growth in current USD. Real growth, domestic prices and currency changes jointly determine that outcome.</p><div class="table"><table><thead><tr><th>Series / observation</th><th>Period and unit</th><th>Treatment</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>UBOS preliminary nominal GDP: 250,447; prior 227,875</td><td>FY2025/26 and FY2024/25; UGX billion</td><td>Official preliminary release; keep fiscal periods explicit</td></tr><tr><td>URBRA commentary: 30.7 trillion</td><td>FY2024/25, UGX</td><td>Dated commentary; another official page assigns a different fiscal label</td></tr><tr><td>URBRA commentary: 35.6 trillion</td><td>March 2026, UGX</td><td>Later dated stock; do not add to the earlier stock or NSSF assets</td></tr><tr><td>Article GDP/GCF and comparator averages</td><td>Inherited historical series</td><td>Retain as scenario/archive until annual observations and metadata reconcile</td></tr></tbody></table></div><p>Refresh protocol: retrieve annual WDI GDP (NY.GDP.MKTP.CD), GCF share (NE.GDI.TOTL.ZS), and real growth (NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG), plus UBOS vintage tables. ## 3. Funding sources, uses and additionality

<p>The article’s illustrative incremental 2030 source allocation totals $7.2bn: banks 0.8, pensions 0.4, CIS/other 0.3, private equity/credit 0.3, public/UDB 0.6, diaspora 0.2, FDI 2.1, DFI/climate 1.2 and offshore private capital 1.3. These are targets to challenge, not available funds. Assign each investor one source category; disclose overlaps between a DFI commitment, a bank loan and a guaranteed bond.</p><p>For a UGX100bn transaction financed by sponsor equity 20, DFI subordinate capital 20 and pension debt 60, sources equal 100. If 30 repays existing financing, only 70 is new productive expenditure before national-accounts adjustments. Report the pension’s 60 as participation, not another 60 of project cost. Separate commitments, disbursements, refinancing, domestic/imported capital goods and contingent guarantees. Use a project ID and instrument ID to reconcile each ledger.</p>

## 4. Institutional architecture and decision rights

<div class="table"><table><thead><tr><th>Alternative</th><th>Benefit</th><th>Principal difficulty</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>A. A proposed National Capital Formation Council would coordinate dependencies; it would not replace statutory regulators or investor committees. MoFPED is a proposed policy host, UDB a possible implementation anchor, Oakstone a possible strategy/structuring/PMO adviser, and Old Mutual a contemplated manager/adviser. None is represented here as appointed. GCIFP is a product-development concept, not a licence or committed investor.</p><p>The policy sponsor approves scope and public expenditure; the preparation committee authorises studies; a licensed manager recommends investments; each investor’s committee decides its own capital; regulators supervise within statutory powers. A regulator’s observer role requires legal review and cannot imply endorsement. Procurement, conflicts and fee approvals must be recorded separately.</p>

## 5. Two preparation lanes and gates

<p>Use one lane for existing operating assets and receivables, another for greenfield projects. The first tests verified cash flow and refinancing; the second must resolve development, land, permits, construction, demand and completion risks before long-term savings are exposed.</p><div class="table"><table><thead><tr><th>Gate</th><th>Required evidence</th><th>Decision / spending control</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>0 Strategic fit</td><td>Sponsor, ownership, public need, exclusions</td><td>Small screening budget; register conflicts</td></tr><tr><td>1 Viability</td><td>Demand, indicative cash flow, legal route, sponsor capacity</td><td>Approve bounded feasibility scope or reject</td></tr><tr><td>2 Bankability</td><td>Technical design, permits, land, ESG, risk allocation</td><td>Independent study review; stop if critical risks unresolved</td></tr><tr><td>3 Investment readiness</td><td>Term sheets, diligence, model, security and disclosure</td><td>Manager recommendation; no implied investor approval</td></tr><tr><td>4 Independent approval</td><td>Investor mandate, risk limits, valuation and legal opinions</td><td>Each authorised committee decides; conditions precedent</td></tr><tr><td>5 Close and monitor</td><td>Executed documents, funded equity, security perfection</td><td>Release only against conditions; ongoing covenants</td></tr></tbody></table></div><p>Every gate has a named risk owner, cost ceiling and kill memo. Study expenditure is sunk if a project fails. An illustrative funnel of 40 expressions of interest, 25 early rejections, 15 Gate-1 reviews, five detailed diligences, two committee submissions and one close is a workload scenario, not a conversion forecast.</p>

## 6. Products and fiduciary safeguards

<div class="table"><table><thead><tr><th>Product</th><th>Illustrative horizon</th><th>Precondition</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Seasoned infrastructure debt</td><td>5–12 years</td><td>Audited cash flow, debt-service resilience, enforceable security</td></tr><tr><td>Private credit</td><td>2–6 years</td><td>Borrower controls and redemption matched to asset liquidity</td></tr><tr><td>Receivables / ABS</td><td>Pool-dependent</td><td>True sale, servicing continuity, reserves and trigger waterfall</td></tr><tr><td>Operating energy platform</td><td>Asset life</td><td>Offtaker, tariff, maintenance and currency risk allocation</td></tr><tr><td>Rental property</td><td>Long term; later pilot</td><td>Title, independent valuation, occupancy and exit evidence</td></tr><tr><td>Greenfield infrastructure</td><td>10+ years</td><td>Specialist construction equity and completion support</td></tr><tr><td>Private equity / venture capital</td><td>7–10 / 10+ years</td><td>Loss-bearing mandates, manager capability and realistic exits</td></tr></tbody></table></div><p>Product horizons are design assumptions, not Ugandan regulatory limits. Pension assets must match benefit obligations, liquidity and concentration constraints. No daily redemption should be promised against illiquid assets without separately funded liquidity arrangements.</p><p>Minimum safeguards cover independent valuation and challenge, qualified custody, related-party restrictions, transparent all-in fees, ESG and grievance processes, leverage and concentration limits, liquidity stress testing, external audit and incident reporting. Investment permission is not a duty to invest. Guarantees reduce specified risks but leave exclusions, enforcement delays, basis risk and guarantor counterparty exposure.</p>

## 7. Worked underwriting challenges

<p>All amounts below are illustrative UGX billion unless stated otherwise. They are arithmetic tests, not transaction quotations.</p><div class="table"><table><thead><tr><th>Test</th><th>Base</th><th>Stress / implication</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Debt service coverage</td><td>CFADS 20 / debt service 15 = 1.333x</td><td>20% cash-flow haircut: 1.067x; 30%: 0.933x</td></tr><tr><td>Credit loss on a 100 portfolio</td><td>PD 5% × LGD 40% × 100 = 2</td><td>PD 15% × LGD 60% × 100 = 9; a junior 10 buffer is nearly consumed</td></tr><tr><td>Net yield before guarantee</td><td>16% coupon − 2% expected loss − 1% fees − 1% liquidity/hedge = 12%</td><td>With a 2% guarantee fee and loss reduced to 0.5%, net = 11.5%</td></tr><tr><td>Capital stack / uses</td><td>20 equity + 20 subordinate + 60 senior = 100</td><td>30 refinance leaves 70 new expenditure; never report 180 mobilised</td></tr></tbody></table></div><p>A guarantee can improve tail protection while lowering expected net yield. ## 10. UHIF and recycling sequence

<p>The historical UHIF concept combines early risk capital, preparation of candidate note issuers and later refinancing through investment products. Its 2019 $1bn ambition, 15–21% gross return range and $10–25m technical-assistance envelope are legacy design assumptions, not current fundraising terms or expected returns.</p><p>Keep technical-assistance grants, risk-bearing equity and senior investment instruments in separate accounts. Compare a closed-end fund, corporate holding vehicle, separately managed mandate and issuer-by-issuer structure before selecting the legal form. Tax, licensing, investor eligibility, valuation, insolvency, custody and redemption require local counsel. An open-ended ambition does not justify on-demand liquidity.</p><p>Pilot A: operating asset refinancing of 100 with 70 senior, 20 DFI and 10 equity. Pilot B: a 60 receivables pool with 42 senior, nine mezzanine and nine retained risk, subject to true sale, servicing and waterfall triggers. Pilot C: sponsor/DFI capital bears greenfield risk, followed by pension refinancing only after operating evidence and independent approval. None is a proposed commitment by the named institutions.</p>

## 11. Commercial model and preparation economics

<p>Keep the public framework available for inspection and feedback. Commercialise services that require execution responsibility: institution-specific diagnostics, training, project screening, feasibility coordination, structuring, licensed investment management where authorised, monitoring and implementation support. Open analysis builds trust; paid mandates must define scope, deliverables, procurement, liability and conflicts.</p><p>Potential payers are policy sponsors for programme design, grant providers for public-good preparation, project sponsors for agreed studies, investors for mandated diligence and licensed funds for approved management services. Disclose retainers, recoverable advances and success fees; do not charge conflicting parties without informed approval. Do not accept capital or sell investment products through this website.</p><p>The paper’s $0.4–0.8m diagnostic and $2–5m setup budgets are preliminary planning ranges requiring scoped bids. A preparation cohort of ten studies costing one unit each plus four advanced studies costing four units each costs 26 units before overhead. If only one closes, full cost recovery would require 26 units from that close before other receipts. A viable project may not bear that charge. Grants or sponsor contributions must explicitly fund public benefits, failed studies and timing risk; recycling is a partial recovery mechanism, not perpetual financing.</p><p>Measure cash received, contractual receivables, grant restrictions, failed-project write-offs, overhead and recovery lag separately. The repository facility model demonstrates negative cash when delayed recoveries do not cover ongoing outlays; it is a sensitivity tool, not a business valuation.</p>

## 13. Accountability, negotiation and evidence gates

<p>A public dashboard should show projects by gate, study spend, rejection reasons, time to decision, commitments versus cash, new expenditure versus refinancing, guarantee exposure, defaults, recoveries, fees and conflicts. Aggregate confidential data where needed; retain source records for independent verification.</p><p>Negotiation redlines: no compelled pension allocation; no regulator endorsement by implication; no guarantees described as free; no uncapped study recovery; no related-party selection without disclosure and approval; no illiquid asset with unsupported redemption promises; no success fee on unclosed commitments; no confidential client records in the public repository.</p><p>Before investor solicitation obtain current legal opinions on URBRA/CMA permissions, offering rules, public procurement/PPP authority, tax, true sale, security perfection, insolvency and beneficial ownership. Before publishing credentials obtain client consent and independent substantiation. The source experience materials inform the emphasis on preparation, structuring, infrastructure and implementation, but unverified transaction values, client endorsements and detailed deal records are deliberately not republished. This is a sanitized synthesis, not an audited track record.</p>

## 14. Work together on a specific item

<p>Choose an item or chapter, identify your audience and role, describe the contribution offered, evidence available, expected outcome and any constraints. Use the existing <a href="index.html#collaborate">interest form</a> to prepare an email, or open a public item-specific issue. The form opens an email draft; it does not store or submit your interest until you send it.</p><p>A proposed intake workflow is: acknowledge interest, check consent and conflicts, match it to a workstream owner, agree a bounded task, then record an outcome. No automatic matching or response-time commitment is claimed. Public issues are for non-confidential feedback; private mandates require separate agreement.</p>
