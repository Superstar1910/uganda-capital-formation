# Arrears, creditworthiness and market infrastructure
Prior project work connected disputed/public arrears and loans in court to weak financing. Historic headline amounts remain unverified and are not adopted here.

Before restructuring or securitisation: verify creditor, legal entitlement, dispute status, budget authority, payment schedule and affordability. Model true sale/security and recourse; prevent duplicate claims and financing. A securitisation cannot cure an invalid claim.

Market infrastructure extends beyond dispute resolution: credit information; beneficial ownership; collateral registries; land rights; custody/depository; settlement; valuation; corporate disclosure; ratings/credit assessment; insolvency/workouts; arbitration; securitisation/true sale; issuance/distribution; investor literacy. Record existing functionality before proposing another institution.

Mineral-linked investments: test shareholder governance, future capex obligations, cash-flow ring fencing, security/step-in, environmental liabilities and lifecycle certainty. No current mining-law conclusion is made without primary legal review.
