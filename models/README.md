# Model contracts
## Strategy laboratory
Constant nominal USD growth from approximate strategy starting point to target. GDP output, scenario investment and difference conditional on the same path. No causal relationship assumed.
## Article path
Annual piecewise linear GDP and investment shares: 2025 USD62bn/22.65%, 2030 USD120bn/30%, 2035 USD250bn/35%, 2040 USD500bn/40%. Comparison fixed at 24%. Includes 2026–2040 only. This reproduces the September article, not an official GDP forecast or independently refreshed historical release. Cumulative amounts differ slightly from rounded article headlines.
## Facility economics
USD millions; synthetic parameters. 10 studies/year × USD0.1m, 20% close rate, full direct successful-project cost recovery after 2 years, USD0.5m overhead and USD1m annual grant. Fractional closings are expectation values. A negative cash balance is an uncovered funding need, not permitted borrowing. Replace with cohort-level schedules and procurement quotes for decisions. Costs of failures do not vanish. Outstanding recoveries after year five are not counted as current cash.
## Mobilisation ledger
Sources equal uses; investor shares are components, not extra totals. Refinancing is separate from new capex. A guarantee is recorded in fiscal/risk ledgers, not as an extra funding source unless actually paid. New productive capex still needs national-accounts reconciliation.
