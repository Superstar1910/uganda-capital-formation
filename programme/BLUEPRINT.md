# Uganda National Capital Formation Programme — integrated research edition
Design • Structure • Mobilise • Implement

## Purpose
Translate Uganda’s US$500bn by 2040 ambition into prepared productive assets, appropriate financing instruments and measurable economic outcomes. Treat the incomplete intermediation architecture as a hypothesis to test against savings supply, investor demand and asset readiness; do not assume capital is abundant or freely deployable.

## Operating architecture
MoFPED is the proposed policy host. A proposed National Capital Formation Council coordinates and monitors; it has no regulatory or investor approval powers. The earlier design contemplated Finance Minister chair, BoU Governor vice-chair and joint MoFPED–BoU–CMA technical secretariat. These are design proposals, not appointments.
UDB is the proposed execution and capital-formation anchor. Old Mutual appears in prior project design as potential fund manager/adviser; appointment is not evidenced and professional management remains subject to mandate, eligibility and competitive governance. Oakstone’s proposed role covers strategy, structuring, financial modelling, transaction advisory and implementation PMO. GCIFP informs the candidate note issuer and infrastructure product-development concept; any engagement must be documented.

## Three engines
1. Project preparation creates diligence-ready assets.
2. Risk allocation places defined risks with capable sponsors, guarantors, insurers and catalytic investors.
3. Anchor capital establishes price, governance and demonstration transactions.

A small market-development unit, a ring-fenced preparation facility and separately governed investment products are distinct functions. Trustees and licensed managers retain voluntary investment decisions; regulators do not endorse individual investments or guarantee performance.

## Seven platforms
Hybrid infrastructure; UG EXIM; infrastructure bonds; VC/innovation; guarantee coordination; carbon/environmental markets; DUGX/tokenised securities. Each addresses a different function and requires its own legal, operating and risk case. The platform list does not constitute approved policy, an offer or secured finance.

## Sector focus
Use ATMS priorities for origination: agro-industrial development; tourism development; mineral-based development including oil and gas; science, technology and innovation including ICT and creative industries. Enabling energy, water, transport, housing, digital and industrial infrastructure support these sectors. National priority never substitutes for investment merit.

## Implementation cycles
2026–2030: build and test institutions and first instruments. 2031–2035: scale proven pipelines, managers and markets. 2036–2040: expand productive capacity and capital recycling. This harmonises the later article/graphic cycles; earlier decks used alternative boundaries, preserved in the conflict register.
A national 90-day design sprint and a retirement-capital 100-day diagnostic are related but distinct work programmes. Neither authorises fundraising or pension subscriptions.

## Outcome ledger
Record funding sources once, investor participation separately, new productive uses separately, and operational outcomes after commissioning. Track jobs quality, exports/FX, value added, tax, retirement beneficiary outcomes, market liquidity and fiscal risk. Do not add stocks, annual flows and payments turnover.
